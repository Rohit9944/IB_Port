Ensuring Trusted and Secure Maritime Partnerships

IBPortDash provides a comprehensive pre-vetting system designed to verify the credibility and reliability of companies involved in the ship chartering process. By analyzing operational history, industry data, and platform insights, IBPortDash helps users identify trustworthy partners and minimize business risks.

Our proprietary verification process assigns each counterparty a Safety Score, allowing cargo owners, ship operators, and brokers to make informed decisions before entering into charter agreements.
