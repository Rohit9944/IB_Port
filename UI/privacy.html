Introduction

IBPortDash values your privacy and is committed to protecting your personal information. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and safeguard your data when you use our website and appliance care services.

By accessing or using IBPortDash, you agree to the terms described in this Privacy Policy.

Information We Collect

We may collect personal information such as your name, email address, phone number, and account credentials when you register or interact with our platform.

Additionally, we may collect appliance information, service history, warranty details, and usage analytics to provide better service recommendations.

How We Use Your Information

We use the collected information to operate and improve our services, manage appliance records, send service reminders, and enhance the overall user experience.

Your information also helps us analyze usage patterns and improve predictive maintenance recommendations.

Information Sharing

IBPortDash does not sell or rent your personal information to third parties. However, we may share limited data with trusted service partners, payment providers, or legal authorities when required.

All third-party services are required to maintain strict confidentiality standards.

Data Security

We implement industry-standard security measures to protect your personal data from unauthorized access, misuse, or disclosure.

Despite these measures, no system can guarantee complete security, and users should take precautions when sharing personal information online.

Cookies & Tracking Technologies

Our website may use cookies and similar technologies to enhance user experience, analyze traffic, and personalize content.

You can control cookie preferences through your browser settings.

Your Privacy Rights

You may request access, correction, or deletion of your personal data at any time by contacting our support team.

Depending on your location, you may also have additional rights under privacy laws such as GDPR or CCPA.

Policy Updates

We may update this Privacy Policy periodically to reflect service changes, legal updates, or improvements to our platform.

Any updates will be posted on this page with the revised date.
